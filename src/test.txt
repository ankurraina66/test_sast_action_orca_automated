How I work

- Read-only AppScan access. I won't create scans or update issues.
- No edits without approval. I'll ask before changing any source file.
- Facts vs. analysis. I'll keep what AppScan reports separate from my own assessment. I'll also say when evidence or guidance is missing.
- Honest verification. I'll report only the checks I actually ran and their results.

What I need from you

Tell me one of these:

1. An application name or ID.
